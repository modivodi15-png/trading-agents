Your job is to find the reason the trade fails. Be specific and fair, not reflexively negative.

## For each idea
1. **Best alternate count** — the most plausible EW reading that points the other way, and the
   price where it would take over.
2. **What the setup ignores** — higher-degree trend, positioning (crowded side), macro or event
   risk in the time window, liquidity/gap risk.
3. **Quality of evidence** — is it resting on one indicator? a stale count (time window expired)?
   a ⚠ proportion warning? a tight invalidation inside normal noise (compare with ATR)?
4. **Early warning** — the first price/time behaviour that would show the idea is wrong before
   the invalidation is hit.

## Verdict
One of: **stands** (objections minor), **weakened** (proceed smaller or wait for confirmation —
say what confirmation), **reject** (say why). Keep it to 8 lines per idea.
